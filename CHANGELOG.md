# Changelog

All notable changes to the "BearTheme" extension will be documented in this file.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-09-11

### Added
- **10 Brand New Striking & Concept Themes**:
  - `Bear Cyberpunk Neo-Tokyo (Light)` — Clean sci-fi cyberpunk light theme.
  - `Bear Terracotta & Clay (Light)` — Organic editorial terracotta, olive & petroleum.
  - `Bear Solar Flare (Light)` — High-contrast polar radiant sunlight & deep cobalt.
  - `Bear Lavender Cloud (Light)` — Dreamy vaporwave lilac mist & retro candy.
  - `Bear Terminal 90s (Light)` — Vintage tractor paper & retro phosphor green.
  - `Bear Obsidian Blood (Dark)` — Pure obsidian with radiant crimson, scarlet & antique gold.
  - `Bear Matrix Acid (Dark)` — Radioactive cyber matrix & toxic acid lime.
  - `Bear Deep Abyss (Dark)` — Deep ocean bioluminescence with neon cyan & electric coral.
  - `Bear Volcanic Magma (Dark)` — Basalt black rock with fiery magma orange & sulfur yellow.
  - `Bear Synthwave Sunset 84 (Dark)` — Outrun 80s arcade sunset with neon fuchsia & sunset orange.
- New official minimalist Nordic bear silhouette icon logo.
- The extension now proudly features **23 complete themes** covering every developer style!

## [1.2.0] - 2026-09-11
